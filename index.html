About

A bit about me

I’m a software engineer building products that make everyday work easier. I recently worked as a founding engineer on an AI-enabled clinic management platform, and I’m exploring AI-powered learning tools through my personal project, Study AI Tools.

I work end to end, from understanding user needs and translating them into features to implementation and refinement based on feedback. I have a strong backend focus, but I’m comfortable working across the stack to deliver complete features. I care about removing unnecessary constraints, automating repetitive work, and keeping software reliable and maintainable without removing the human judgment that makes it useful.

I’m currently exploring AI integrations to understand where AI can meaningfully help with human work and where human involvement remains important. Beyond engineering, I’m reflective and try to understand situations from different perspectives. To me, empathy is fundamental to how we work with others and build things for them.
